description: "Test a strategy safely, then understand what changes when a run can use real funds."

Miramarket gives you two available ways to run a strategy. **Simulate** runs the strategy without placing real trades. **Run Live** can use real funds when its conditions trigger, on Polymarket prediction markets, tokenized stocks on Base, and perps on Hyperliquid.

In the builder, the run control sits in the canvas's top-left corner, next to the starting amount. It starts on **Simulate**; **Run Live** is in its menu. The same control appears on featured strategies and on strategies the [assistant](/strategy-builder/assistant) builds.

<img src="/images/tutorial/run-options.webp" alt="Run menu with Simulate and Run Live shown" />

Live trading availability also depends on your region. If it is unavailable, the run menu shows **Run Live** as disabled and Simulation is your only option.

## Simulation

Use **Simulate** before you use **Run Live**. Sign in first; a simulation does not use your funds. It validates the strategy, saves a simulation version, and starts a simulated run without placing real trades.

Before you start, review:

- The market and named outcome, stock, or perp.
- The selected `YES` or `NO` side, or `LONG` or `SHORT` and leverage for a perp.
- Each condition and its threshold or timing.
- Each action and its allocation.
- The starting amount, from `$5` to `$10,000`.

Simulation follows the settings you supplied. It can reveal an incomplete or invalid strategy, but it cannot decide whether those settings match your trading idea.

A simulation is more forgiving than a live run. For example, a simulated perp order fills at the midpoint price with no spread, trading fees, or funding.

## Run Live

**Run Live** is available. Use it only when you are ready to use real funds. The run menu keeps **Run Live** unavailable until you sign in and your account finishes setting up, and it can also stay unavailable where live trading is not supported in your region.

Before a live run starts, Miramarket asks you to confirm. Live trades do not necessarily happen immediately; the strategy can attempt them when its conditions trigger. Execution and fills are not guaranteed.

## Sign in and add funds

Use **Sign in** in the top bar when you are ready to run a strategy. Miramarket sets up your account, powered by [Privy](https://privy.io).

Once you are signed in, the account menu shows your **Cash** and **Portfolio**, and holds **Deposit**, **Withdraw**, **Referrals**, and **Sign out**.

Add funds from **Deposit**:

<img src="/images/tutorial/deposit-modal.png" alt="Add funds dialog with Pay with card, Transfer from an exchange, and Transfer from a wallet shown" />

- Pay with card
- Transfer from an exchange
- Transfer from a wallet

Adding funds does not start a live run; you must separately choose and confirm **Run Live**.

Signing in and adding funds does not mean you should run live immediately. Review the strategy first.

A strategy moves money where it needs it. If a stock or a perp needs funds on another network, Miramarket moves them for you when the strategy runs. Your **Cash** includes your free balance on Hyperliquid, and **Withdraw** draws on it automatically.

## Safe workflow

1. Build the strategy, or have the assistant build it.
2. Check every market, side, condition, and allocation.
3. Run a simulation.
4. Review the result and revise the strategy if needed.
5. Read the [live-run checklist](/strategy-builder/validation).
6. Add funds.
7. Use **Run Live** only if you accept the risk.

## When not to run live

Do not run live if:

- You do not understand the market.
- You are unsure whether `YES` or `NO`, or `LONG` or `SHORT`, is correct.
- You do not understand how leverage, funding, and liquidation affect a perp.
- The price, close time, or active recurring window looks wrong.
- You have not tested with **Simulate**.
- You are not comfortable losing funds.

## While a run is active

The builder locks the canvas while an active run is in progress. Follow the run from **Strategies** in the top bar.

You can still change the steps that have not run yet. Open the strategy from **Strategies**, select **Edit**, make your changes, then select **Save changes**. Steps that have already run stay as they are, and so do the settings of a condition the run is waiting on. Review the run before you change future steps or start another run.
