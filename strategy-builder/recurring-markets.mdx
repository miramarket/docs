Recurring markets are market families that repeat on a schedule. The **Recurring** filter groups them by time family, including **5m**, **15m**, **1h**, **4h**, and **daily**.

<img src="/images/tutorial/recurring-markets.png" alt="Recurring markets filter with time options and market cards shown" />

## Choose an active window

1. Open **Market** in the left panel.
2. Select **Recurring**.
3. Select a time family.
4. Read the market card and confirm that its time window is current.
5. Choose the named outcome and its `YES` or `NO` side.
6. Click or drag the card to add the recurring market action.

The builder refreshes a recurring action to match the current or next available market in its family. Do not assume it will continue forever. Review the active market and the strategy state before each live run.

## What to check

- The time family you selected.
- The current market window and close time.
- The named outcome and `YES` or `NO` side.
- The condition and allocation that apply to the recurring action.
- Whether the market is still active.

## Use a demo first

Recurring markets can change quickly. Use **Run Demo** to make sure the canvas, selected market, and timing match your intent before you use **Run Live**.

For a first recurring strategy, use one market action and one condition. Add more nodes only after you understand how the current window is selected.
