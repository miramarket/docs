Recurring markets are market families that repeat on a schedule. Examples include 5-minute, 15-minute, hourly, 4-hour, or daily markets.

<img src="/images/tutorial/recurring-markets.png" alt="Recurring markets filter with time options and market cards highlighted" />

## When to use recurring markets

Use recurring markets when your idea applies to a repeated event.

Examples:

- Short-window crypto up/down markets.
- Daily market families.
- Repeated event markets with similar wording.

## How to find them

1. Open **Market**.
2. Choose **Recurring**.
3. Pick a time family such as **5m**, **15m**, **1h**, **4h**, or **daily**.
4. Choose a market card and outcome.

## What to check

Recurring markets can change quickly. Before running live, check:

- The current time window.
- The market close time.
- Whether you selected `YES` or `NO` correctly.
- Whether the strategy should continue into the next repeated market.

## Start with demo

Use **Run Demo** before using real funds. Recurring markets can move fast, so a demo run is especially useful.
