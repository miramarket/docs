An action is what the strategy does after a condition is met.

<img src="/images/tutorial/simple-strategy-canvas.png" alt="A simple strategy with a price condition and market action highlighted" />

## Buy a market outcome

The most common action is buying a `YES` or `NO` outcome.

Before using this action, check:

- The market question.
- The chosen `YES` or `NO` side.
- The current price.
- The amount or allocation.

## Hold

Use hold when you want the strategy to keep the current position instead of moving funds.

## Move to wallet

Use a wallet action when the strategy should move funds away from the current market path.

This can be useful for exits, stop-loss style strategies, or after a market resolves.

## Allocation

Allocation is the percentage of available funds sent to an action.

Example: if the strategy has $100 available and an action has 70% allocation, that action uses $70.

If you split between two actions, make sure the split matches your intent.

## Demo vs live

Actions in **Run Demo** are simulated.

Actions in **Run Live** can use real funds. Always demo first.
