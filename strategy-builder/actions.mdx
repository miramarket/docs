An action is a node from the **Market** tab. It tells the strategy what to do after a condition continues.

Use the **Market** tab for ordinary markets, recurring markets, **Wallet**, and **Utility**. Click a card to add it, or drag it onto the canvas to choose its position.

## Compare actions

| Action | What it does | Common use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Buy Market** | Buys the selected `YES` or `NO` side. | Enter a market position. |
| **Wallet** | Directs its allocation to your wallet destination. | Reserve part of a split. |
| **Hold** | Keeps a market position open or leaves funds in the wallet. | Wait for a later rule without changing that allocation. |

## Buy a market outcome

Market cards create a **Buy Market** action. Select a named outcome when the card has more than one, then choose `YES` or `NO`. Click or drag the card to add that choice to the canvas.

Review the action node before you run:

- The market question and named outcome.
- The selected `YES` or `NO` side.
- The displayed price.
- The allocation.

## Use a recurring market outcome

**Recurring** is a market filter, not a separate action type. It creates the same market-outcome action, but tracks a recurring market family. Choose a time family such as **5m**, **15m**, **1h**, **4h**, or **daily**, then choose the current market and side.

Verify the active window before you run. See [Recurring markets](/strategy-builder/recurring-markets) for details.

## Wallet

Choose **Wallet** in the **Market** tab to add a Wallet action. A Wallet action directs its allocated funds to the selected wallet destination.

<img src="/images/tutorial/wallet-action.png" alt="Wallet action card in the Market tab shown" />

**Connect** and a Wallet action are different:

- **Connect** signs you in with a wallet so that **Run Live** can become available.
- A Wallet action is a node in the strategy itself.

## Hold

Choose **Utility** in the **Market** tab to add **Hold**. Hold keeps the current position open and lets the strategy continue to child conditions.

<img src="/images/tutorial/utility-hold.png" alt="Hold card in the Utility section shown" />

Use Hold only when you understand the position that the strategy will keep. A Hold node may follow a market action and continue the strategy to another condition.

**Hold** can mean two different things:

- After a market action, it keeps that market position open.
- In a wallet path, it leaves the allocation in the wallet.

## Allocation

Allocation is the percentage of the available amount assigned to an action. A single action receives `100%` by default.

If a condition has multiple actions, their allocations must total `100%`. The builder balances new actions evenly unless you set an allocation explicitly.

Example: a 70% market action and a 30% Wallet action split the available amount between those two actions.

## Build a valid flow

- Start with one root condition or one root market action.
- Place market actions after the condition they depend on.
- Add a condition below a market action when the strategy should continue after that action.
- Use the canvas placement guides when dragging to avoid adding a node in the wrong place.

## Simulation vs live

Actions in **Simulate** are simulated. **Run Live** can use real funds when conditions trigger.

Always review the action, side, and allocation in a simulation before using **Run Live**.
