An action tells the strategy what to do after a condition continues. Market actions come from the **Market** tab. **Exit** and **Hold** are under **Utils** in the **Condition** tab. Click a card to add it, or drag it onto the canvas to choose its position.

## Compare actions

| Action | Where to find it | What it does | Common use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Market action | **Market** tab | Buys the selected `YES` or `NO` side, buys a stock, or opens a `LONG` or `SHORT` perp position. | Enter a position. |
| **Exit** | **Condition** tab, **Utils** | Moves its allocation out of the current position and back to your wallet. | Take profit, stop a loss, or keep part of a split out of the market. |
| **Hold** | **Condition** tab, **Utils** | Keeps a position open, or leaves funds where they are. | Wait for a later rule without changing that allocation. |

## Buy a market outcome

Prediction market cards create a market action. Select a named outcome when the card has more than one, then choose `YES` or `NO`. Click or drag the card to add that choice to the canvas.

Review the action node before you run:

- The market question and named outcome.
- The selected `YES` or `NO` side.
- The displayed price.
- The allocation.

[Stock](/strategy-builder/markets#tokenized-stocks) cards add a **Buy** action for that stock. [Perp](/strategy-builder/perps) cards add a `LONG` or `SHORT` action with its own leverage setting.

## Use a recurring market outcome

**Recurring** is a market category, not a separate action type. It creates the same market-outcome action, but tracks a recurring market family. Choose a time family such as **5m**, **15m**, **1h**, **4h**, or **daily**, then choose the current market and side.

Verify the active window before you run. See [Recurring markets](/strategy-builder/recurring-markets) for details.

## Exit

**Exit** moves its allocation out of the strategy's current position and back to your wallet.

- After a market action and a condition, Exit closes that share of the position: a take-profit when the condition waits for a gain, or a stop-loss when it waits for a fall. For a perp, it closes the position and returns the margin plus or minus your profit or loss.
- In a split, Exit keeps that share of the available amount in your wallet instead of trading it.

**Sign in** and an Exit action are different:

- **Sign in** sets up your account, which is required before you can run a strategy.
- An Exit action is a node in the strategy itself.

Signing in and adding funds does not start a live run. See [Simulation and Live Runs](/strategy-builder/run-simulation-live) for the sign-in and funding flow.

## Hold

**Hold** keeps the current position open and lets the strategy continue to child conditions.

Use Hold only when you understand the position that the strategy will keep. A Hold node may follow a market action and continue the strategy to another condition.

**Hold** can mean two different things:

- After a market action, it keeps that market position open.
- In a split, it leaves that share of the available amount where it is.

## Allocation

Allocation is the percentage of the available amount assigned to an action. A single action receives `100%` by default.

If a condition has multiple actions, their allocations must total `100%`. The builder balances new actions evenly unless you set an allocation explicitly.

Example: a 70% market action and a 30% Exit split the available amount between those two actions.

For a perp, the allocation is the margin. Leverage then multiplies the size of the position. See [Perps](/strategy-builder/perps#set-the-leverage).

## Build a valid flow

- Start with one root condition or one root market action.
- Place market actions after the condition they depend on.
- Add a condition below a market action when the strategy should continue after that action.
- Use the canvas placement guides when dragging to avoid adding a node in the wrong place.

## Simulation vs live

Actions in **Simulate** are simulated. **Run Live** is available and can use real funds when conditions trigger. Execution and fills are not guaranteed.

Always review the action, side, and allocation in a simulation before using **Run Live**.
