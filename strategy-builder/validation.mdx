title: "Live-run checklist"
description: "Review every part of a strategy before using Run Live."
---

Use this checklist after a successful simulation and before **Run Live**, which is available for Polymarket prediction markets, tokenized stocks, and perps. It is a review of your intent, not a guarantee of execution or fills.

## Check the market

- Does the market question match your idea?
- Do you understand how the market resolves?
- Is the market still active?
- Is the close time or recurring window acceptable?

## Check the outcome

- Did you choose `YES` or `NO` correctly, or `LONG` or `SHORT` for a perp?
- Does the selected named outcome and side match the result you want?
- Is the current price acceptable?

## Check the condition

- Is the condition type correct?
- Is the direction correct, such as greater than or less than?
- Does the threshold use the intended side and comparison?
- Does a time condition use the intended close time or specific time?
- Is a stock or perp threshold a price in dollars, not a probability?
- Is a short's stop-loss above the entry price, and a long's below it?

## Check the action

- Does each action do what you expect?
- Is the allocation correct?
- If you use **Exit** or **Hold**, is that intentional?

## Check a perp

- Is the leverage what you intend? Leverage multiplies losses as well as gains.
- Is every stop-loss closer to your entry than the liquidation price? For a stock, index, commodity, FX, rates, or pre-IPO perp that is roughly `1 ÷ leverage` away. A crypto perp's depends on your whole Hyperliquid balance and other open crypto positions, so check its actual liquidation price.
- Does each perp order reach the `$10` minimum: its amount times its leverage?
- Are you comfortable paying funding for as long as the position may stay open?

## Run a simulation first

Use **Simulate** before **Run Live**. A simulation can show an invalid or incomplete strategy and lets you review the behavior without real trades.

## Understand the risk

Signing in and adding funds does not start a live run. You must separately choose and confirm **Run Live**.

If the [strategy assistant](/strategy-builder/assistant) built the strategy, or you started from a [featured strategy](/strategy-builder/featured-strategies), review it like any other strategy. Its markets, thresholds, and allocations are what the assistant or the author chose, not automatically what you intend.

Live trading can lose money. Prices can move, a market can close, a leveraged perp can be liquidated, and execution or fills can fail or differ from what you expect.

Only use **Run Live** when you are comfortable with those risks.
