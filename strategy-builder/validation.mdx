---
title: "Before you run live"
description: "Review a strategy before using real funds."
---

Use this checklist before **Run Live**.

## Check the market

- Does the market question match your idea?
- Do you understand how the market resolves?
- Is the market still active?
- Is the close time acceptable?

## Check the outcome

- Did you choose `YES` or `NO` correctly?
- Does the selected side match the result you want?
- Is the current price acceptable?

## Check the condition

- Is the condition type correct?
- Is the direction correct, such as greater than or less than?
- Is the threshold reasonable?
- Does a time condition use the right close time or trigger time?

## Check the action

- Does the action do what you expect?
- Is the allocation correct?
- If funds move to wallet, is that intentional?

## Run demo first

<img src="/images/tutorial/run-options.png" alt="Run menu with Run Demo and Run Live highlighted" />

Use **Run Demo** before **Run Live**. Demo mode helps you catch wrong markets, wrong sides, and wrong rules.

## Understand the risk

Live prediction market trading can lose money. Prices can move, liquidity can change, execution can fail, and a strategy can behave differently than expected.

Only use **Run Live** when you are comfortable with those risks.
