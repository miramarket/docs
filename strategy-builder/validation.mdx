---
title: "Live-run checklist"
description: "Review every part of a strategy before using Run Live."
---

Use this checklist after a successful demo and before **Run Live**. It is a review of your intent, not a guarantee that a market will move or execute as you expect.

## Check the market

- Does the market question match your idea?
- Do you understand how the market resolves?
- Is the market still active?
- Is the close time or recurring window acceptable?

## Check the outcome

- Did you choose `YES` or `NO` correctly?
- Does the selected named outcome and side match the result you want?
- Is the current price acceptable?

## Check the condition

- Is the condition type correct?
- Is the direction correct, such as greater than or less than?
- Does the threshold use the intended side and comparison?
- Does a time condition use the intended close time or specific time?

## Check the action

- Does each action do what you expect?
- Is the allocation correct?
- If you use **Wallet** or **Hold**, is that intentional?

## Run demo first

<img src="/images/tutorial/run-options.png" alt="Run menu with Run Demo and Run Live shown" />

Use **Run Demo** before **Run Live**. A demo can show an invalid or incomplete strategy and lets you review the behavior without real trades.

## Understand the risk

Live prediction market trading can lose money. Prices can move, a market can close, and an action can fail to execute as you expect.

Only use **Run Live** when you are comfortable with those risks.
