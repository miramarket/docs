---
title: "Build a strategy"
description: "Create a Miramarket strategy from a market, condition, and action."
---

A strategy says what should happen when a market reaches a rule you choose.

Example: "If this market trades above 60 cents, buy `YES`."

## Step 1: Pick the outcome

Open the **Market** tab. Choose a market, then choose whether the strategy should buy `YES` or `NO`.

## Step 2: Add a condition

Open the **Condition** tab and choose a rule.

Use **Price Condition** when you care about the current market price.

Use **Time Condition** when you want something to happen before close or at a specific time.

Use **Resolution** when the next step depends on whether a market resolves `YES` or `NO`.

## Step 3: Add an action

The action is what the strategy does after the rule is met. Common actions are buying an outcome, holding, or moving funds back to a wallet.

## Step 4: Check the canvas

<img src="/images/tutorial/simple-strategy-canvas.png" alt="A simple strategy with a price condition and market action highlighted" />

Read the strategy from left to right or top to bottom:

1. The condition checks the rule.
2. The action runs if the rule is met.
3. The allocation shows how much of the available amount goes to the action.

## Step 5: Run a demo

Click **Run Demo** before going live. Demo mode helps you check the strategy without placing real trades.

## Step 6: Go live carefully

Use **Run Live** only after you check the market, outcome, amount, and condition. Live runs can use real funds.
