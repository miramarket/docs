## The building blocks

Every strategy combines:

- A market and selected `YES` or `NO` side.
- A condition that decides when the strategy continues.
- One or more actions, including a market outcome, **Hold**, or **Wallet**.
- An allocation for each action.

## Build in the right order

1. Choose a market and side in **Market**.
2. Add a condition from **Condition**.
3. Add the action that follows the condition.
4. Review the canvas, including all allocations.
5. Use **Simulate** before **Run Live**.

Click a palette card to add it near the selected node, or drag it to place it precisely on the canvas.
