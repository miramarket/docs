Markets are the questions your strategy can trade or watch.

## Use the Market tab

Open **Market** in the left panel.

<img src="/images/tutorial/market-panel.png" alt="Market tab with filters and search shown" />

Use the filters to browse:

- **Trending** for active markets.
- **Recurring** for markets that repeat.
- Topic categories such as **Politics**, **Sports**, **Crypto**, **Finance**, **Tech**, and **Weather**.
- Search when you know what you want.

## Read a market card

<img src="/images/tutorial/market-card-outcomes.png" alt="A market card with YES and NO outcome buttons shown" />

A market card usually shows:

- The market question.
- One or more outcomes.
- `YES` and `NO` buttons.
- Prices for each side.
- Volume or activity information.

## Choose `YES` or `NO`

Choose `YES` if you want the strategy to buy the side that wins when the event happens.

Choose `NO` if you want the strategy to buy the side that wins when the event does not happen.

Check this carefully. Choosing the wrong side changes the strategy.

## Search for a market

Use the search box to narrow the list. Search by topic, team, asset, person, or event.

If you cannot find the right market, do not use a similar-looking one unless you understand how it resolves.

## Add a market to the canvas

Click a market card to add it to your strategy. You can then connect it with conditions or use it as an action.
