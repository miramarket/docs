title: "Markets and outcomes"
description: "Find a market, choose the right outcome, and add it to a strategy."

Markets are what a strategy can trade. The **Market** tab has three kinds:

- **Prediction Markets**: questions on Polymarket, the supported prediction market venue, with `YES` and `NO` sides.
- **Stocks**: tokenized stocks on Base. They are not prediction markets and behave differently (see [Tokenized stocks](#tokenized-stocks)).
- **Perps**: perpetual futures on Hyperliquid, with `LONG` and `SHORT` sides and optional leverage. See [Perps](/strategy-builder/perps).

A market action attempts to buy what you selected when you run the strategy; execution and fills are not guaranteed.

## Use the Market tab

Open **Market** in the left panel. The search box sits at the top, then the three kinds, then the categories for the kind you chose.

<img src="/images/tutorial/market-panel.webp" alt="Market tab with the search box, the Prediction Markets, Stocks, and Perps tabs, and the Prediction Markets categories" />

Under **Prediction Markets**, use the categories to browse:

- **Trending** for active markets.
- **Recurring** for markets that repeat.
- Topic categories such as **Politics**, **Sports**, **Crypto**, **Finance**, **Tech**, **Culture**, **Geopolitics**, **Economy**, **Weather**, and **Elections**.
- **Live** for markets on events happening now.
- Search when you know what you want.

On a phone or tablet, the categories form one row you can swipe.

## Read a market card

<img src="/images/tutorial/market-card-outcomes.webp" alt="A market card with YES and NO outcome buttons shown" />

A prediction market card can show:

- The market question.
- One or more outcomes.
- `YES` and `NO` buttons.
- A displayed price for each side.
- Volume information.

## Choose an outcome and side

Some market cards contain several named outcomes. For example, a card may list several teams or people. In that case, make two choices:

1. Select the named outcome you mean.
2. Select `YES` or `NO` for that outcome.

`YES` means you choose the side for that named outcome happening. `NO` means you choose the side for that named outcome not happening. Read the market question and its resolution details before choosing either side.

## Grouped events and NegRisk markets

Some events group mutually exclusive named outcomes in one card. You may see this called a NegRisk market. It is a convenient way to compare related outcomes, such as several teams, candidates, or award nominees.

| Choice | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Named outcome | The specific team, person, or option you selected. |
| `YES` | You choose that selected outcome happening. |
| `NO` | You choose that selected outcome not happening. It does not automatically select a particular alternative. |

For example, if you select **Team A**, `YES` means Team A wins. `NO` means Team A does not win; it does not mean Team B wins unless you select Team B separately.

<img src="/images/tutorial/market-card-outcomes.webp" alt="Grouped market card with YES selected on the Google outcome" />

Read the event's resolution details before you add a grouped outcome. A group can contain similar-looking choices with very different meanings.

## Search for a market

Use the search box to narrow the list. Search by topic, team, asset, person, or event. Under **Stocks** and **Perps**, the box searches that list instead.

If you cannot find the right market, do not use a similar-looking one unless you understand how it resolves.

## Tokenized stocks

Choose **Stocks** in the **Market** tab to see tokenized stocks on Base: real equities wrapped as tokens. The list includes Apple, Amazon, Alphabet, Meta, Microsoft, Micron, NVIDIA, Palantir, Tesla, and others, each with a `c` suffix on its symbol (AAPLc, NVDAc, TSLAc). They are not prediction markets, and they behave differently in a strategy:

- A stock has no `YES` or `NO` side. You either hold it or you do not, so a stock action simply buys it.
- Its price is in **dollars per share**, not cents or a probability. A price condition on a stock uses a dollar threshold, for example "if TSLA crosses above $400".
- It is bought and sold as a token swap on Base rather than as a Polymarket order, so the price you pay is the pool's price, which can differ from the price of the underlying share, most of all in thin trading and outside US market hours.
- A stock never resolves. A time or resolution condition cannot watch it, and a stock you still hold when the strategy finishes is sold at that point.
- Tokenized stocks are not offered in every region. If they are unavailable where you are, a strategy that buys one cannot be run live.

<img src="/images/tutorial/stocks-panel.webp" alt="Stocks tab listing Apple, Amazon, Alphabet, and Meta with their ticker and live price" />

Each stock card shows the live pool price and a price history you can switch between one day, one week, one month, and all time. There is no side to pick: click the card to add a **Buy** action for that stock, or drag it onto the canvas. A card with no price cannot be added until a price is available. The [strategy assistant](/strategy-builder/assistant) understands stocks too: "buy TSLA if it crosses $400 and sell at $450" builds a complete stock strategy.

Stocks and [perps](/strategy-builder/perps) are different products. **Stocks** buys a tokenized share on Base. Some stocks are also listed under **Perps**, where you can go long or short with leverage on Hyperliquid.

## Add a market action to the canvas

For a prediction market, selecting `YES` or `NO` prepares the card; a [tokenized stock](#tokenized-stocks) card has no side and is added directly; a perp card adds a `LONG` unless you click `SHORT`. Click the card to add the selected market action, or drag the card to choose a position on the canvas.

When the card appears on the canvas, verify the market, named outcome, selected side, and allocation. The action defaults to `100%` when it is the only action in its decision.

## Before you add a market

- Make sure the market question matches your idea.
- Check how and when the market closes or resolves.
- Confirm the displayed side is the one you intend to buy.
- Do not substitute a similar-looking market if you do not understand its resolution.
