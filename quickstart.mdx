This guide shows the fastest way to build a simple strategy and test it with **Run Demo**.

## 1. Open the strategy builder

Go to [app.miramarket.org](https://app.miramarket.org).

<img src="/images/tutorial/builder-overview.png" alt="Miramarket strategy builder with the top bar, market panel, and canvas shown" />

1. The top bar has **New Strategy**, **Run Demo**, **Run Live**, and **Connect**.
2. The left panel lets you choose markets, conditions, wallet actions, and utility actions.
3. The canvas is where your strategy appears.

## 2. Choose a market

Open the **Market** tab. Use **Trending**, a category filter, or the search box to find a market.

<img src="/images/tutorial/market-card-outcomes.png" alt="A market card with YES and NO outcome buttons shown" />

Pick the outcome you want the strategy to buy. Most markets show `YES` and `NO` prices.

## 3. Add a condition

Open the **Condition** tab and choose **Price Condition**.

<img src="/images/tutorial/condition-panel.png" alt="Condition tab with Price Condition, Time Condition, and Resolution shown" />

A condition tells the strategy when to act. For your first strategy, use a price rule such as "if the price is above 50 cents."

## 4. Add the market action

Click a market card to add it to the strategy. The canvas shows the condition and the market action.

<img src="/images/tutorial/simple-strategy-canvas.png" alt="A simple strategy with a price condition and market action shown" />

The condition is the rule. The action is what happens if the rule is met.

## 5. Run a demo

Click **Run Demo**.

<img src="/images/tutorial/top-bar-run-demo-connect.png" alt="Top bar with Run Demo and Connect shown" />

A demo run does not place real trades. Use it to check whether the strategy behaves the way you expect.

## 6. Go live only when ready

Use **Connect** to sign in with a wallet. Then use the run menu to choose **Run Live**.

<img src="/images/tutorial/run-options.png" alt="Run menu with Run Demo and Run Live shown" />

Live runs can use real funds. Review the market, outcome, amount, and condition before running live.
