title: "Build your first strategy"
description: "Create a simple strategy and test it with Run Demo."
---

This guide helps you create one small strategy and test it with **Run Demo**. You do not need to use real funds.

## What you will build

You will create a strategy with one rule and one action:

> When the price condition is met, buy the `YES` or `NO` side you selected.

The exact market can change over time. Choose an active market you understand and read its resolution rules before adding it.

## 1. Open the builder

Go to [app.miramarket.org](https://app.miramarket.org).

<img src="/images/tutorial/builder-overview.png" alt="Miramarket strategy builder with the top bar, market panel, and canvas shown" />

1. The top bar includes **New Strategy**, **Run Demo**, the run menu, and **Connect**.
2. Use **Condition** and **Market** in the left panel to find nodes.
3. The canvas is where the strategy appears.

## 2. Choose a market and side

Open the **Market** tab. Use **Trending**, a category filter, or the search box to find a market.

<img src="/images/tutorial/market-card-outcomes.png" alt="A market card with YES and NO outcome buttons shown" />

Some cards contain several named outcomes. First select the named outcome you mean. Then select `YES` or `NO` for that outcome. Selecting a side prepares the card; click the card to add it to the canvas, or drag it to choose its position.

Check the market question, the named outcome, the selected side, and the displayed price before continuing.

## 3. Add a price condition

Open the **Condition** tab and choose **Price Condition**.

<img src="/images/tutorial/condition-panel.png" alt="Condition tab with Price Condition, Time Condition, and Resolution shown" />

A condition tells the strategy when to act. Click the card to add it at the current position, or drag it onto the canvas to place it precisely.

For a first strategy, use a simple threshold such as "price is greater than `50¢`." Configure the market, side, comparison, and threshold in the condition node. Make sure the condition watches the market and side you intend.

## 4. Add the market action

Return to **Market**. Choose the market and side again, then click or drag its card onto the canvas. Place it after the condition.

<img src="/images/tutorial/simple-strategy-canvas.png" alt="Price condition and market action on the Miramarket canvas shown" />

The action is what the strategy does when its condition is met. A single action uses a `100%` allocation by default. If you add multiple actions to the same decision, review their allocations so they total `100%`.

## 5. Read the canvas before you run

Check each node in order:

1. The condition names the price, time, or result that the strategy checks.
2. The action names the market outcome, **Hold**, or **Wallet** destination that follows.
3. The allocation shows how the available amount is divided between actions.

If the strategy is not what you meant, edit the node or use **New Strategy** to start a fresh draft.

## 6. Run a demo

Click **Run Demo**.

<img src="/images/tutorial/top-bar-run-demo-connect.png" alt="Top bar with Run Demo and Connect shown" />

A demo run does not place real trades. It validates the strategy and starts a simulated run. Review the market, side, condition, and allocation first. A simulation follows the settings you gave it; it cannot tell you whether those settings match your intent.
