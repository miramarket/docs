Miramarket helps you build prediction market strategies without writing code. A strategy combines a market, a condition, and one or more actions on a visual canvas.

Start with **Run Demo**. It simulates a strategy without placing real trades. Use **Run Live** only after you understand every node in the strategy and are prepared to risk funds.

## How a strategy works

1. Choose a market and the side you want to buy, such as `YES` or `NO`.
2. Add a condition that decides when the strategy should continue.
3. Add an action, such as buying a market outcome, using **Hold**, or moving funds to **Wallet**.
4. Review the canvas, then use **Run Demo** before considering **Run Live**.
