Miramarket helps you build trading strategies without writing code. A strategy combines markets, conditions, and actions on a visual canvas, or you can describe one in plain English and let the strategy assistant build it.

A strategy can trade:

- Prediction markets on Polymarket, the currently supported prediction market venue.
- Tokenized stocks on Base, such as Tesla, NVIDIA, and Apple.
- Perps (perpetual futures) on Hyperliquid, long or short, with optional leverage.

The live builder and **Run Live** are available. Start with **Simulate**, which runs a strategy without placing real trades. Use **Run Live** only after you understand every node and are prepared to risk funds. Execution and fills are not guaranteed.

## How a strategy works

1. Choose a market and the side you want, such as `YES` or `NO`, or `LONG` or `SHORT` for a perp.
2. Add a condition that decides when the strategy should continue.
3. Add an action, such as buying a market outcome, **Exit**, or **Hold**.
4. Review the canvas, then use **Simulate** before considering **Run Live**.

## Open the app

The app is at [app.miramarket.org](https://app.miramarket.org). It opens on the home page, where you can ask the strategy assistant for a strategy or open a featured one. Choose **Builder** in the top bar to open the canvas at `app.miramarket.org/builder`.

Signing in sets up your Miramarket account. Adding funds does not start a live run; you must separately choose and confirm **Run Live**.

Use the website at [miramarket.org](https://miramarket.org) to learn what Miramarket does and launch the app.

Trading prediction markets, tokenized stocks, and perps involves risk, and leverage can magnify losses. Miramarket is not financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.
