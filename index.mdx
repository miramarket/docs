Miramarket helps you build prediction market strategies without writing code. You choose markets, add simple rules, and test what the strategy would do before using real funds.

Start with **Run Demo**. Use **Run Live** only when you understand the strategy and are ready to risk funds.

<img src="/images/tutorial/landing-launch-app.png" alt="Miramarket website with the Launch App button shown" />

## What you can do

- Open the strategy builder from **Launch App**.
- Browse prediction markets from the **Market** tab.
- Choose a `YES` or `NO` outcome.
- Add conditions such as price, time, or resolution rules.
- Add actions such as buying an outcome, holding, or moving funds to a wallet.
- Test a strategy with **Run Demo** before using **Run Live**.
